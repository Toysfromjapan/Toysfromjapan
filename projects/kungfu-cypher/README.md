# Kung Fu Cypher (working title)

Short series. A character in a mythic early China where battles are fought as underground rap cyphers, shot like a 1980s Hong Kong kung fu cult film.

## Rules of the world
- Mythic, not historical. Borrow freely from early China (Shang to Han) but never let accuracy beat the look.
- Rural China on the surface, an underground battle scene beneath it. Every arena is a real rural space repurposed for a cypher.
- No modern objects on screen: sound comes from drums, gongs, bells, clapping, stomping and voice.

## Look (to be locked with the user)
- 1980s Hong Kong studio kung fu: painted sky backdrops, soundstage courtyards, saturated primaries, colored gel light (red, green, amber), crash zooms and whip pans, 35 mm grain.
- Underground battle rap: tight crowd circle, handheld energy, smoke, single hard top light, call and response.
- Rural China: rammed earth, timber, thatch and tile, paddies, loess terraces, lantern light.

## Locations
Each location gets a bible entry in `bible/locations/` built from photo references in `references/`. Candidate arenas, to confirm:
1. Round earth fortress courtyard (Fujian tulou style): a ready-made circular arena with tiered balconies for the crowd.
2. Village threshing floor at night: packed-earth circle, lanterns on poles, haystacks as seating.
3. Cellar under an ancestral hall: timber posts, candle niches, low ceiling, dust in the light.
4. Dry riverbed amphitheater below karst peaks: rock ledges as stands, bonfire center.
5. Teahouse back room: lattice screens, steam, gamblers' tables pushed aside.
