# Reference shopping list

What to collect for each location, and where to look. Collect stills you can upload (your own photos, scans, or saved images for private reference). Aim for 4 to 8 per location: one wide, one material close-up, one light reference, one crowd or scale reference.

Tag each upload with the location number from `README.md` and what it is for: shape, material, light, or crowd.

## Light and texture to shoot yourself
Your GFX100S is ideal for texture plates. Useful captures:
- Rammed earth, weathered timber, clay roof tiles, straw, bamboo, river stone: flat light, close up, for material reference.
- Paper lanterns and candles in a dark room: how the warm light falls off.
- Smoke or haze with one hard light from above: the cypher key light.
- A crowd circle from eye level and from above, even with friends, for blocking.

## Mood words (use in prompts once locked)
Painted sky, soundstage courtyard, red and green gel light, lantern glow, haze, packed earth, rammed-earth walls, timber balconies, crowd circle, gong and drum.
